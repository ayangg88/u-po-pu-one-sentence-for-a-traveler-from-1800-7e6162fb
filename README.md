One sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800. The sentence stands alone: no words coined after the traveler's time, no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their head — a road of light joining every house on earth.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (149 characters, one sentence).
